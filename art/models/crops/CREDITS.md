Changes: wheat textures scaled to 2048 px; leaves drawn with cut-out alpha; the maize
is shown part by part as it grows (roots hidden, tassel and ears later); the tomato
plant is split into stem, leaves, flowers and fruit by colour, its stem slimmed, its
fruit thinned out, and several copies make the potato and eggplant bushes. Unripe
wheat is tinted green. `PlantModels` bakes one plant per crop
and stage (`tools/bake_tools.gd`). Each folder keeps its original `license.txt`.
